LAGOS SERVICES — HOMEPAGE DESIGN SPEC
======================================

STATUS: LOCKED
PHASE: 0.4 — Design homepage

PURPOSE
-------
The homepage must immediately support the two primary MVP actions:
1. Find a service.
2. List a service.

LOCKED STRUCTURE
----------------

1. HEADER
   Logo → /
   Services → /services
   Categories → /categories
   List Your Services → /list-your-services
   Search → service search experience

2. HERO
   Headline:
   "Find someone who can get it done."

   Supporting line:
   "Skilled people. Real services. Lagos."

   Primary interaction:
   Prominent service search input

   Placeholder:
   "What service do you need?"

   Helper examples:
   "Try 'plumber', 'makeup artist', 'generator repair'…"

3. CATEGORIES
   Heading:
   "Browse services"

   Display:
   6–8 initial categories

   Action:
   "View all categories"

   Example categories:
   Plumbing
   Electrical
   Cleaning
   Repairs
   Beauty
   Photography
   Catering
   Construction

   Note:
   These are examples only. Actual categories will be sourced from Supabase.

4. SERVICE LISTINGS
   Heading:
   "Services people are offering"

   Service cards contain:
   - Image when available
   - Service title
   - Provider name
   - Lagos/location
   - Price/rate when supplied
   - View Service action

   Example:
   Generator Repair & Maintenance
   Chinedu Okafor
   Lagos
   From ₦25,000
   View Service

   Rule:
   Discovery remains service-focused while provider identity is clearly visible.

5. PROVIDER CTA
   Heading:
   "Have a service to offer?"

   Supporting text:
   "Put your services in front of people looking for them."

   Primary action:
   "List Your Services"

6. FOOTER
   Keep simple and useful.

   Potential links:
   Lagos Services
   Services
   Categories
   List Your Services
   About
   Contact
   Privacy
   Terms

MOBILE LAYOUT
-------------
1. Logo + menu
2. Hero
3. Search
4. Categories
5. Service cards
6. Provider CTA
7. Footer

MVP BOUNDARIES
--------------
Do NOT add:
- Giant map
- Reviews/ratings
- Top-provider rankings
- Complex filters
- Pricing marketplace
- Fake testimonials/statistics
- Login/signup dominating the header
- AI recommendations
- Huge promotional banners
- Excessive CTAs

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
-----------------
- Mobile-first
- Clear hierarchy
- Search is the primary user action
- "List Your Services" is the primary provider CTA
- Keep the homepage useful without requiring login
- Use real approved service data when available
- Do not invent social proof or marketplace statistics
- Provider profiles are discovered through services, not primary navigation
