LAGOS SERVICES — NAVIGATION SPECIFICATION
============================================

STATUS: LOCKED

PURPOSE
-------
This document defines the MVP navigation for Lagos Services.


1. DESKTOP HEADER
-----------------

Primary header:

Logo → /
Services → /services
Categories → /categories
List Your Services → /list-your-services
Search → service search experience

Navigation principle:
- Keep the header simple.
- Make service discovery prominent.
- Make "List Your Services" the primary provider CTA.


2. MOBILE HEADER
----------------

Mobile header:

Logo
Menu button

When opened:

- Services
- Categories
- List Your Services

Search remains prominent on the homepage and services/discovery
experience rather than being buried in the menu.


3. SEARCH
---------

Search is a primary product action.

The homepage should prominently present a service search field.

Example:

"Find someone who can get it done."

[ What service do you need?                         Search ]

Search should also be readily available on the services/discovery
page.


4. PROVIDER NAVIGATION
----------------------

There is no "Providers" item in the primary navigation.

Reason:
Users primarily arrive with a service need.

The intended discovery path is:

Need
  ↓
Service
  ↓
Provider
  ↓
Other services from that provider

Provider profiles remain first-class public pages and are reachable
through service listings.


5. FOOTER
---------

The footer may contain:

Lagos Services
- Services
- Categories
- List Your Services
- About
- Contact
- Privacy
- Terms

Secondary pages such as About, Contact, Privacy, and Terms are not
necessarily part of the initial implementation and should only be
built when required.


6. PRIMARY ACTIONS
------------------

For service seekers:
- Search for a service
- Browse categories
- Open a service
- Contact provider

For providers:
- List Your Services


7. NAVIGATION RULE
------------------

Do not add navigation items merely because a page exists.

Every primary navigation item should support one of the locked
primary user journeys.

Keep the MVP navigation intentionally small.
