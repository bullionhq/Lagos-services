LOCKED HOMEPAGE
---------------
1. Header
   - Logo
   - Services
   - Categories
   - List Your Services
   - Search

2. Hero
   - Headline: "Find someone who can get it done."
   - Supporting line: "Skilled people. Real services. Lagos."
   - Prominent service search input
   - Placeholder: "What service do you need?"
   - Helper examples: "Try 'plumber', 'makeup artist', 'generator repair'…"

3. Categories
   - Heading: "Browse services"
   - Show 6–8 initial categories
   - "View all categories" link
   - Example categories may include Plumbing, Electrical, Cleaning, Repairs, Beauty, Photography, Catering, Construction.
   - Final category data will come from Supabase.

4. Service Listings
   - Heading: "Services people are offering"
   - Service cards are service-focused.
   - Card information:
     * Service image when available
     * Service title
     * Provider name
     * Lagos/location
     * Price/rate when supplied
     * View Service action

5. Provider CTA
   - Heading: "Have a service to offer?"
   - Supporting text: "Put your services in front of people looking for them."
   - Primary action: "List Your Services"

6. Footer
   - Keep simple and useful.
   - Core links may include Lagos Services, Services, Categories, List Your Services, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms.

DESIGN BOUNDARIES
-----------------
Do not add to the MVP homepage:
- Giant map
- Reviews/ratings
- Top-provider rankings
- Complex filters
- Pricing marketplace
- Fake testimonials or statistics
- Login/signup as a dominant header action
- AI recommendations
- Huge promotional banners
- Excessive CTAs

MOBILE
------
- Logo + menu
- Hero
- Search
- Categories
- Service cards
- Provider CTA
- Footer

PRODUCT RULE
------------
The homepage is designed around the two primary MVP actions:
1. Find a service.
2. List a service.

Provider identity is visible, but discovery remains service-focused.
Provider profiles are reached through service listings, not the primary navigation.
